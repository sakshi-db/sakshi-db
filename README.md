## 💫 A Little About Me

<h3 align="center"><code>Learning & Exploring</code> 👾</h3>

</div>

I'm a CS student learning Java, DSA, Git, GitHub and all the CS stuff I probably should've known yesterday. Exploring how software actually works beyond the `copy → paste → pray → run` workflow and building a better understanding of programming by actually **building things, breaking things, and fixing them**. <br><br> You can ask me about why I started learning DSA, why every error somehow becomes a 40-minute debugging session, or why I thought *"I'll just learn one more thing"* was a reasonable idea! Outside the code I do a little bit of UI/UX, design, anime, sports, basically any shiny new rabbit hole I can find, and occasionally pretending I have my life figured out. A fun fact about me - I didn't start out as the person who *"always knew she wanted to code."* I got here, decided to learn anyway, and now I'm having way too much fun figuring out what I can build.
